The normal forms at a glance

A quick reference to keep open while you read the case studies.

1NFEvery cell holds one value. No lists or repeating groups inside a column.
2NFIn 1NF, and every non-key column depends on the whole composite key, not part of it.
3NFIn 2NF, and no non-key column depends on another non-key column.
BCNFFor every dependency X → Y, X is a superkey.
